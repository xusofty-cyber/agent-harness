# Decisions

Written decision records. Each entry states the decision, the rationale,
and the rejected alternative, so a later session can see *why*, not just
*what*. Decisions are append-only: never rewrite a past entry to match a
new conclusion — supersede it with a new entry that cites the old one.

- **Decision:** <what was decided>
- **Rationale:** <why — the evidence or constraint that forced it>
- **Rejected:** <the alternative(s) considered and why not>
- **Date:** <YYYY-MM-DD>. <Where it was implemented, if applicable>
